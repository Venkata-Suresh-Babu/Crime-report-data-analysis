# Crime Report Analysis

- This repository contains a comprehensive data analysis of crime reports from 2020 to the present, visualized through an interactive Power BI dashboard. 

- The project focuses on identifying trends, high-risk areas, and victim demographics to provide actionable safety suggestions.

## Project Overview

- **Tool Used:** Power BI  

- **Dataset:** Crime Data from 2020 to Present  

- **Total Crime Reports:** 752.91K  

- **Primary Objective:** To analyze crime distribution patterns across geographic locations and timeframes to improve public safety protocols.

## Key Data Insights
- **Gender Disparities:** Data indicates a higher number of female victims (590.23K) compared to male victims (311.96K)

-  **Temporal Trends:**
    -  Vehicle theft occurs more frequently on weekdays.
    -  Overall crime rates peaked in 2020, saw a decline in 2022 during the pandemic, and slightly increased again in 2023.

-  **Geographic Hotspots:** The Pacific, Southeast, and Southwest regions recorded the highest crime rates.

-  **Victim Demographics:** The average age of victims is approximately 40 years.

-  **Common Modalities:** Cutting instruments are among the top weapons used by criminals.

## Dataset Features
**The analysis utilizes a rich dataset containing the following key attributes:**

- **Temporal:** Date Reported, Date of Occurrence.

- **Geographic:** Area Name, Area Code, and specific Location addresses.

- **Victim Profile:** Age, Gender (F, M, X), and Descent.

- **Crime Details:** Crime Codes/Descriptions, Premises type, and Weapon details.

- **Status:** Current status of the crime report.

## Approach
- **Data Loading:** Imported the raw "Crime_Data_from_2020_to_Present.csv" file.  

- **Data Cleaning:** Utilized Power Query to preprocess the dataset, ensuring accuracy in reporting districts and victim information.  

- **Visualization:** Developed an interactive dashboard featuring bar charts for crime types, geographic maps for location tracking, and trend lines for yearly analysis.

## Recommendations & Suggestions

- **Enhanced Monitoring:** Install trackers on vehicles to mitigate the high rate of weekday thefts.

- **Targeted Safety:** Implement specific safety protocols for individuals under the age of 40.

- **Resource Allocation:** Expand surveillance and survey coverage in the identified "Top 10" high-crime areas like Pacific and Southwest.

- **Focus Areas:** Increase security measures on weekdays and develop specific protection programs for female residents.
